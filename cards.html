4. Chronological review

Check every date and time. You can correct dates, start/end times and overnight endings here. Review checks do not import anything. Times are Sydney clock previews; UTC/DST event conversion is not implemented yet.

5. Card OCR details

Correct the colour category or shift label if needed. A printed time range overrides defaults. N = 19:00 to 07:00 next day; N10 = 21:45 to 07:45 next day. Light green means Educator; darker green means floor staff. Both use an 8-hour A/P default when no time range is printed, starting at 07:00/13:30. Printed times override that. Verify that OCR has not missed a printed range. These are clock-time previews until a date and timezone are confirmed.

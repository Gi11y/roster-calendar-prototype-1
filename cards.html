7. Card OCR details

Correct the colour category or shift label if needed. A printed time range overrides defaults. N = 19:00 to 07:00 next day; N10 = 21:45 to 07:45 next day. Light green means Educator; darker green means floor staff. Both use an 8-hour A/P default when no time range is printed, starting at 07:00/13:30. Printed times override that. Verify that OCR has not missed a printed range. Every shift still needs your review. Sydney offsets and elapsed duration are calculated once its date is confirmed.

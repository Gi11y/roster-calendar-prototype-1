Keep Roster handy

iPhone

Open Roster in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. If you opened it inside another app, use its menu to open the link in Safari first.

Android

Open Roster in Chrome. Open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Use it anywhere

Open the site once while online and let it finish loading. Reading screenshots can then work offline. Connecting Google and saving calendar changes need an internet connection.

Your screenshot stays on your device. Review every date and hour before saving.

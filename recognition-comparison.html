Method and limitations

Four full rosters and two Educator crops. Expected values were checked against the screenshots and confirmed shift rules. Tests ran one at a time on the same laptop/browser, with the same pinned OCR engine and model assets. The standard Educator card warmed assets first. Full-roster reader order alternated.

Both readers retained all dates and staff roles. The baseline's two missing September letters were reproduced here. Both readers misread two end times as 18:30 rather than 19:30 in another roster. This result does not establish phone accuracy or timing, or real Google Calendar behaviour.

Screenshots and raw OCR stayed local. No Google sign-in or calendar writes were used. The restoration build passed 130 tests, production build and offline checks.

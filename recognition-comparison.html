How to compare

  1. Choose the exact same screenshot in each reader, one after the other.
  2. Count detected cards, correct dates and correct shift labels separately.
  3. Compare printed overrides and default hours against the screenshot.
  4. Record wrong automatic values separately from entries flagged for correction.
  5. Record processing time shown under About this prototype. Repeat on a phone before making performance claims.

No Google sign-in is needed. Screenshots and results stay on the device. No real roster imports are enabled.

Roster Calendar

Prototype 1 v0.2 - Google sign-in and event checks

Unofficial test app. Not affiliated with NSW Health. Use a personal Google account. No roster images are processed in this prototype.

1. Connect Google

Google's sign-in script is loaded only after you choose to connect. The first tap prepares it; the second tap opens the sign-in popup.

Permission requested: calendar.app.created only.

2. Create your Roster calendar

Stores the returned ID in IndexedDB on this device, not on our server.

3. Insert one test event

Synthetic event: Roster Prototype 1 Test, 3 November 2026, 12:00–12:30 in Australia/Sydney. No real shift data. Repeated taps reuse an identical test event. Extra copies from older versions are shown before you choose to remove them.

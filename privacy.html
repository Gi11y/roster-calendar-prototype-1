Roster

Your privacy

Your screenshot never leaves your phone. We only change the Roster calendar this app creates.

Your screenshot

Reading, cropping and recognising shifts happen on your device. We do not upload screenshots, use a cloud image service, run analytics, or use your data for advertising or AI training.

Connecting Google

When you choose Google features, Google handles sign-in and calendar requests. We read calendar names so you can find your Roster calendar. We do not read events from your other calendars.

Inside the app-created Roster calendar, we read shifts to show changes and save only changes you confirm: dates, hours, titles, reminders and shift identifiers. Changing a shift changes Google Calendar only; it does not notify your workplace.

What stays on your device

The current screenshot, recognised text and reviewed shifts stay in memory while the page is open. The app remembers your selected calendar, a setup identifier if calendar creation was interrupted, and whether the install hint has been shown. App files are saved for offline use. We do not save a long-term Google sign-in credential.

Where data goes

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives normal website requests, and Google receives the sign-in and calendar requests you choose to make. Screenshots are not part of those requests. We do not sell your data or send it to another roster service.

Google information is used only for these calendar features, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

You can disconnect at any time, remove this app's access in your Google Account, delete its Roster calendar in Google Calendar, or clear this site's saved data through your browser. Google Calendar keeps the shifts you have already saved until you change or delete them.

Questions

Contact the person who shared this app with you. You can also use the project support page; keep screenshots and private roster details out of public issues.
